BatchNorm, and what it averages over

Every normalisation layer does the same arithmetic — subtract a mean, divide by a standard deviation, then rescale with γ and β. The only thing that changes between BatchNorm, LayerNorm, InstanceNorm and GroupNorm is which numbers get pooled into one mean. Hover any cell: everything outside its pooling group is greyed out, and the maths is written underneath.
